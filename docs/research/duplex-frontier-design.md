# Seeker — Frontier Design Briefing: Making "Duplex" Actually Work

*Synthesis of 18 scored proposals, an authoritative building-block verification pass, and a frontier critic. Where the verification and proposals conflict, verification wins. Where I cite Seeker code, I re-checked it against the repo this pass (file:line are real).*

---

## 1. The design target, sharpened

"Make duplex work for Seeker" does **not** mean a talking model. Seeker never speaks. Strip away the conversational baggage and the architecturally valuable property is a **listen-anytime / act-anytime transducer with a silent side channel**. Concretely, a winning design must deliver four things at once:

- **A. Continuous, no-turn listening.** The input PCM stream is never chopped into VAD micro-turns to *decide*. There is no endpointing gate between "heard" and "may act." Today Seeker fakes this with `automaticActivityDetection.silenceDurationMs: 50` (gemini_session.py:116–117) — a turn gate abused as a ~20 Hz re-eval clock. The honest version removes the gate, not shortens it. (Note: the obvious "just disable VAD" move is a **landmine** — `automaticActivityDetection.disabled:true` on Gemini 2.5 native-audio hangs after `ActivityEnd` and dies with a 1011 keepalive timeout. The no-turn-gate property must come from the local/cascade path or OpenAI Realtime `turn_detection:null`, never from Gemini manual-VAD.)

- **B. A sparse, precise, silent action channel.** The channel is empty ~99% of frames and emits exactly once at the right semantic instant — roughly **30–90 fires across a 60–90 min session, i.e. ~0.1% positive frames**. The central enemy is the *trigger-happy / always-respond bias* baked into dialogue-tuned models by RLHF. The winning move, repeated across the strongest proposals, is to **move sparsity out of the model** (where it is a prior you fight) and **into either an explicit policy or a purpose-built rare-event objective** (where it is a knob you tune or a loss you optimize).

- **C. Reliable slide-index semantics at sub-second latency, with override.** The emitted index must be a real, in-range, *local* slide, dispatched through Seeker's existing `evaluate_trigger` guards (tracking.py:49 — bounds / no-op / locality / operator-yield, all pure and model-agnostic) and the `/v1/status/slide` reconcile loop (daemon.py:366). **One sharp correction the critic forces:** that safety net is **reactive and poll-based** — the reconcile loop is a `while True: await asyncio.sleep(interval)` (daemon.py:388–389) that re-anchors *only after* an on-screen change is observed, and the locality guard only bounds *wild* jumps, not *plausible-but-wrong local* ones. So a wrong-but-local slide is **already on the projector** before any correction. Treat the guards as a floor, not the primary precision mechanism.

- **D. Robustness over long, noisy sessions.** Paraphrase, multi-minute tangents, jump-back-to-reread-scripture, repeated text (chorus/refrain), reverberant live-PA, monitor bleed, music-under-speech, multi-speaker. This is where every approach is weakest and least demonstrated.

**Why this reframing matters.** It dissolves the false binary "true full-duplex model (Moshi/Hertz) vs. cascade." Full-duplex-as-conversation is irrelevant; full-duplex-as-architecture (continuous input + always-live action lane) is achievable *today* on a cascade, and is the deployable embodiment of A–D. The frontier question is not "can we get a duplex model" but "**what is the cheapest mechanism that emits a sparse, calibrated, semantically-correct event on a continuous stream — and what frontier work raises the semantic ceiling past what a cascade can reach.**"

A second reframing, owed to the critic, reshapes the whole space: **the four sub-problems are not one problem.** Position-tracking (where am I in the text), boundary-timing (*when* exactly to fire), and on/off-script detection are *different sensors*. Most proposals collapse them into a single "where am I in the manuscript" stream and then discover the timing and chorus cases are unsolved. The highest-leverage ideas below come from *splitting* them.

## 2. The idea space, mapped

**Family 1 — Action-channel-on-backbone (retrain/graft a silent lane).** Reconstruct DuplexSLA's silent, rate-limited tool-call lane on an *open* backbone (Step-Audio-2-mini 7B, Qwen3-Omni, or a surgically-modified Moshi/Mimi RQ-Transformer). The action lives *inside* the model on its native chunk clock. **Trade-off:** the purest A/B fit, but it is genuine ML research — DuplexSLA used ~500k hrs continued-pretraining + ~50k hrs post-training (verification corrects the proposals' 14k figure) and its action channel scores **competitive-with but not above** a plain ASR→LLM cascade (~85.6% vs ~91.3%). You inherit a worse-than-cascade ceiling at frontier cost, with weights unreleased so you re-implement the recipe.

**Family 2 — Frozen-head (Freeze-Omni template).** Freeze a streaming audio encoder (or audio-LLM), tap the per-chunk last-frame hidden state, train only a tiny head emitting `{HOLD, FIRE(index)}`. Cheapest *trained* path. **Trade-off:** the whole bet is one empirical question — *does a frozen hidden state linearly separate slide-boundary-crossing and the correct local index under paraphrase/chorus?* Freeze-Omni proves a frozen head can read a 3-way **dialogue state**; nobody has shown it reading an **N-way manuscript position**. If the states don't separate, the fix (unfreeze) collapses it into Family 1's cost.

**Family 3 — Per-frame posterior (deterministic Bayesian tracker).** Maintain `P(slide=k | audio so far)` every frame with a switching-HMM / particle filter whose observation likelihood fuses streaming-ASR edit-distance (verbatim scripture) + embedding similarity (paraphrase), and whose transition prior encodes forward-bias + penalized jump/repeat. Fire on a MAP-crossing with margin + dwell. **Trade-off:** no training, on-prem, debuggable, best architectural fit to A/B of any *buildable-now* option — but accuracy is hostage to ASR WER (which degrades *both* fused channels at once with no acoustic backstop) and chorus disambiguation rests entirely on the locality prior.

**Family 4 — Inner-monologue repurposing.** Retarget Moshi/Kyutai-STT codebook-0 (the text/"inner monologue" stream that already emits PAD ~65–80% of frames) from words to `{NOOP, ACTION}`, so "mostly silent" is the model's *native prior*. **Trade-off:** the most elegant lens — but verification kills the deployment story: delayed-streams backbones **cannot prime a per-session manuscript** (Kyutai's text prompt is a spelling nudge, not document conditioning), forcing per-deck retraining, and the retarget *throws away the transcript* — the one robust lexical position signal — for a task that is fundamentally lexical position-tracking.

**Family 5 — Cascade-as-duplex (the deployable answer).** Streaming ASR (no TTS, no turn model) → deterministic position sensor → grammar-constrained small tool-LLM only on ambiguity. Silence is *free* (nothing generates speech, so there is no respond-bias to suppress); the no-turn-gate property holds by construction (the only clock is your own timer); the index is structurally valid via constrained decoding + `evaluate_trigger`. **Trade-off:** ASR is the new single point of failure on noisy PA, and per-window statelessness is the wrong shape for repeated text unless you add a persistent position prior. This *is* paradigm (c) from Seeker's own research doc, made concrete.

**Cross-cutting: Training & serving.** (a) **Label factory + eval harness** (forced alignment → frame labels; fire-offset / false-fires-hour / wrong-index / tangent-recovery metrics) — the precondition that makes every other claim falsifiable. (b) **Serving substrate** — a 12.5 Hz Mimi-clocked loop with backpressure, paged-attention KV, StreamingLLM sink-eviction for hour-long sessions; the durable asset a future open action-lane model would slot into. (c) **Sparse-emission objectives** — focal / blank-downweight / Streaming-Anchor-Loss / FastEmit (asymmetric: penalize *early* fires hard, *late* fires mildly) — the toolkit for any trained head.

### The top ~5, in prose

**#1 — Tri-tier cascade (pursue-now).** Streaming ASR (Kyutai STT 1B / Nemotron-Streaming 0.6B / Voxtral-Mini-Realtime) emits committed words with manuscript proper-nouns/scripture pre-loaded as a biasing list. A decision pass runs per committed word over a locality window: rapidfuzz edit-distance (routed to `block_type="scripture"` verbatim text) + embedding cosine (routed to exposition) fuse into a per-candidate score that updates an OLTW/HMM position posterior. Tier-2 fires the argmax directly through `evaluate_trigger` when confident-and-local; Tier-3 escalates only flat/ambiguous cases (chorus) to a small grammar-constrained tool-LLM seeing 2–5 candidates. It scores top on every axis that matters live: **silence is free** (no respond-bias to fight), the no-turn-gate property holds by construction, the index is valid by construction, it is on-prem and cap-free, and it **degrades to HOLD rather than guess** — the correct live failure mode (a missed advance is a nudge; a wrong jump is an embarrassment). The `SlideBlock.block_type` split (manuscript_parser.py:18) hands you the lexical-vs-semantic routing for free. The audio tap is the one piece undersold: the queue is single-consumer drop-oldest (audio_capture.py:125–134), so you add a fan-out, not a share. Residual risk is entirely D (ASR WER on PA, identical-text non-local jumps) — calibration/input problems, not architectural dead-ends.

**#2 — Eval-and-mining harness (pursue-now, do this first).** You cannot train, tune, or even compare sparse-precise emission you cannot measure, and Seeker is currently flying blind: `trigger_latencies` is declared but the latency-recording path is thin (daemon.py:192), the transcript only hits a log file ("Hearing:", gemini_session.py:217), and `evaluate_trigger`'s `max_jump` is effectively unbounded by default. Build the label factory (forced-align recorded sermons → per-slide gold fire-times), the metrics-as-code (signed fire-offset, false-fires/hour on a tangent-heavy held-out set, wrong-index rate, tangent-recovery, chorus-disambiguation), and a deployment logger that co-timestamps transcript + every fire + every `/v1/status/slide` change on **one wall-clock + audio-sample timeline**. Two honest corrections to the original proposal: the logger is **net-new, not "reuse the transcript we already receive"** (it's logged, not persisted with sample offsets), and the "learned confidence threshold" knob it names **does not exist** — native-audio tool calls emit no logprob and `evaluate_trigger` has no such input. Scope the DSPy/textgrad half to prompt-instruction search and build a cached session-replay harness so each trial isn't a 60–90 min live API call.

**#P — Predictive prosodic-boundary sensor (pursue-now; the critic's highest-upside untried idea).** Every one of the 18 proposals collapses audio into a single "where am I in the text" stream. But preachers and worship leaders signal transitions **acoustically and ahead of content**: the pre-line breath, the cadence resolve at a section end, the deliberate pause before scripture, the key-change/downbeat in song mode. A small CPU-cheap detector on log-mel + pitch + energy (pitch-reset, energy-dip-then-onset, speaking-rate inflection, pause-with-intent), run as a **second independent vote**, is the *only* mechanism that can (a) fire ~1 s **before** the line — which worship mode requires and no content-matcher can ever do, because the words arrive *at or after* the moment the slide must already be up — and (b) disambiguate identical-text chorus repeats by **how** the line is delivered, breaking the locality-prior deadlock that every other proposal punts on. It is orthogonal to ASR (degrades independently of WER), trainable without verbatim text labels, and S–M effort. This moves the two hardest sub-problems (predictive song timing; repeated text) from "punted" to "directly attacked."

**#O — Operator-correction-as-teacher (pursue-now; the data answer).** Every trained proposal is starved for frame-aligned labels, and every cheap source (forced alignment) is **noisiest exactly on the hard cases** — jump-back, chorus, paraphrase — i.e. the only cases worth training on. The FOH operator is a live oracle generating precisely those labels, *on-policy*. Reframe Seeker as an apprentice that proposes-but-the-operator-disposes; the stream of (operator advanced **here**, sensor said **there**) corrections is a DAgger-style on-policy dataset that directly fixes the synthetic-to-real distribution shift forced alignment cannot. This converts the field's #1 kill-risk (data scarcity + label noise) into a flywheel. Caveat to validate first: corrections must be **cleanly attributed** (not polluted by the agent's own fires — the reconcile loop's `commanded_index` vs observed distinction at daemon.py:407–421 is the seam to exploit) and operators must actually engage with a propose-confirm UX.

**#3 — PostTrack deterministic posterior (prototype; the principled core of #1).** A switching-HMM/particle filter making `P(slide|audio)` explicit is the cleanest architectural answer to A and B among buildable-now options: the forward recursion runs per ASR partial (no turn gate); silence is flat likelihood the transition prior carries forward; firing is a posterior state-change with an explicit, tunable ROC operating point instead of a dialogue model's respond-bias. It is essentially Seeker's own documented paradigm (c) made fully deterministic. **Fold in the critic's idea M**: add an explicit **OFF-SCRIPT latent state** with its own dynamics and a learned return distribution, so a 3-minute ad-libbed illustration is a *state to be in* (with defined re-acquisition behavior) rather than "low confidence noise" — this is the make-or-break live behavior ("what happens at second 10 vs second 60 of a tangent") that every proposal currently hand-waves. PostTrack is the engine; the prosodic sensor (#P) and operator flywheel (#O) feed it; the cascade (#1) is the same thing with a deterministic readout policy. They are one system.

## 4. A staged build plan

### Stage 0 — Instrument (Now; no training; gates everything)
- **Build:** the eval harness + label factory + deployment logger (#2), with co-timestamped transcript/fires/observed-slide on a wall-clock+sample timeline. Quantify the **cost asymmetry** the whole firing policy depends on (is a 1.5 s-late advance truly invisible? is a one-line-ahead wrong-local slide as bad as a wild jump, or far less?). This is the critic's sharpest question and it determines whether the optimal design is eager-and-reactive or conservative-and-abstain.
- **Proves/kills:** nothing yet — it makes every later claim falsifiable. Without it, all tuning is vibes.
- **Seeker ties:** logger reads `/v1/status/slide` via the existing `get_current_slide_index()` (propresenter_client.py:81) and the reconcile loop's override attribution (daemon.py:407–421).

### Stage 1 — Deployable duplex (Now; no training)
- **Architecture:** Tri-tier cascade (#1) = biased streaming ASR → fused lexical/semantic position sensor → OLTW/HMM posterior (#3, with OFF-SCRIPT latent from M) → Tier-2 direct argmax through `evaluate_trigger`, Tier-3 grammar-constrained small-LLM on ambiguity only. Add the **prosodic-boundary vote (#P)** as a second channel. Wire the **operator propose-confirm flywheel (#O)**.
- **Build:** audio fan-out tap (audio_capture.py queue is single-consumer); ASR worker; the fusion sensor; the cadence/debounce/dwell readout; the manuscript→bias-list builder; the prosodic detector; the Stream-Deck suggest/confirm UX; the attribution-clean override logger.
- **Experiment that proves/kills:** on recorded services with gold fire-times, measure fire-offset, false-fires/hour, wrong-index, chorus-disambiguation, tangent-recovery — **for Tier-1+2 alone first** (the cascade's whole bet is that Tier-3 is rarely needed; measure that before adding the LLM). Then A/B the prosodic vote on song-mode predictive timing and chorus cases specifically. **Kill signal:** if a clean console feed + biasing can't get WER low enough that the deterministic sensor clears the false-fires/hour bar, the cascade ceiling is real and you escalate to Stage 2/3.
- **Critical unweighted variable:** the **audio front-end**. Seeker's own research doc flags that board-send vs room mic + AEC/denoise moves accuracy *more than model choice*. Make a clean console feed + AEC a first-class design element before any model work — measure the front-end's leverage first; it may dissolve the case for the expensive proposals.

### Stage 2 — Near-term prototype (small training / frozen head)
- **Architecture:** Freeze a streaming encoder or audio-LLM; train a tiny `{HOLD, FIRE(pointer-into-local-window)}` head (#4/#5) with a **blank-heavy focal + asymmetric FastEmit loss** (penalize early fires hard, late fires mildly — the genuinely correct, underexploited insight from P2). Critically, **distill from the cascade, not from forced alignment** (the critic's cascade-as-teacher idea): run Stage-1's mature posterior live, use *its* output + operator-confirmed ground truth as soft labels for the head. This sidesteps the forced-alignment-is-garbage-on-hard-cases kill-risk that caps the entire trained-head family and makes the cheap on-device head a *compression of the proven cascade* rather than a fresh research bet.
- **Build:** the head + chunked inference runtime + the distillation pipeline (Stage-1 logs are the corpus). Reuse `evaluate_trigger`/reconcile verbatim — they are pure and model-agnostic.
- **Gating probe (1–2 weeks, run before any pipeline):** the **linear-probe study** — take a frozen Voxtral/Qwen3-Omni/Step-Audio-2-mini, prime a manuscript, run a handful of force-aligned (or operator-labeled) sermons, train a linear/MLP probe on the last-frame hidden state, and measure: (i) is-this-a-boundary-chunk separability, (ii) correct-local-index separability, and (iii) **chorus/re-read confusion specifically**. This single cheap experiment is the shared go/no-go gate for the **entire frozen-head family** (#4, #5, #9, #10, #12). If states separate → graduate to the distilled head. If not (the likely case for chorus/paraphrase) → kill the frozen variant and keep the cascade as the driver, using the encoder's per-chunk confidence merely as an agreement-gating sensor on Stage-1.

### Stage 3 — Frontier bet (original ML; gated on Stage-2 plateau)
- **Architecture:** the **L3 Mimi-clocked serving substrate** (12.5 Hz frame loop, drop-oldest backpressure mirroring audio_capture, CUDA-graphed Mimi, paged-attention KV, StreamingLLM sink-eviction for hour-long sessions) — built *first and independently* because it is the durable asset that survives even if the model on top is thrown away, and it is the only hard-real-time/offline answer to flaky sanctuary wifi. On top: a DuplexSLA-style silent action lane, reached via the cheapest credible route — **Freeze-Omni-style frozen backbone + per-chunk sparse-emission head with Streaming-Anchor-Loss/focal + FastEmit**, trained on the operator+distillation corpus, and only then tuned with **GRPO/RLVR under an asymmetric "fire-once-precisely" reward** if and only if long-horizon sparse-reward RL has been shown to converge (it is an open problem as of mid-2026 — do not put it on a schedule).
- **Experiment that proves/kills:** does the trained action lane beat the Stage-1 cascade on the *measured* hard distribution (paraphrase, chorus, tangent-recovery) by enough to justify GPU ops at the venue? Verification is blunt here: DuplexSLA's own action channel is **competitive-with-not-above** the cascade (~85.6% vs ~91.3%), so a graft can at best approach parity — the *only* honest justification for Stage 3 is a hard requirement the cascade provably cannot meet (true predictive song timing, or fully-offline determinism), not raw accuracy.
- **Budgets, corrected:** assume a **7B** backbone (Step-Audio-2-mini is 7B, not 77B — the proposals misread it), single high-VRAM GPU for inference; do **not** assume "tens of hours suffices" or a single-24GB-GPU on the strength of the misquoted numbers.

## 5. Training & evaluation

**Metrics (build as code in Stage 0; nothing downstream is meaningful without them):**
- **Signed fire-offset (s)** — leads/lags the gold transition; the headline quantity. Requires fire-event and audio on one timeline (the logger's job).
- **False-fires/hour** on a **tangent-heavy** held-out set — the sequence-level quantity frame-loss is blind to; the direct measure of "trigger-happy."
- **Wrong-index rate** — penalized ~5× a late fire (set the weight from the Stage-0 cost-asymmetry measurement, not a guess).
- **Tangent-recovery time** — seconds to re-acquire after an N-minute off-script illustration; the trust-determining behavior nobody specifies.
- **Chorus-disambiguation accuracy** — the case content+locality alone cannot solve; the metric that justifies the prosodic sensor.

**Data pipeline & bootstrapping labels (in order of label quality):**
1. **Operator-correction flywheel (#O) — best, on-policy.** Live (operator-advanced-here, sensor-said-there) corrections, cleanly attributed via `commanded_index` vs observed. The only source that is reliable *on the hard distribution*.
2. **Cascade-as-teacher distillation — sidesteps alignment noise.** Stage-1 posterior + operator ground-truth as soft labels for any trained head.
3. **Forced alignment (WhisperX/NFA/MFA) — volume, but off-policy and noisy on exactly the hard cases.** Use for clean verbatim scripture; distrust on paraphrased exposition (no verbatim anchor → the "dense position surrogate" is itself a fuzzy pseudo-label on ~70% of sermon wall-clock), jump-back, and chorus.
4. **Synthetic TTS (perturbation DSL: paraphrase/tangent/jump_back/repeat_chorus/skip/misquote/crosstalk + live-PA IRs) — volume + hard negatives, but a prosody/acoustic gap that biases fire *timing*** (the graded quantity). Anchor with real hours; the synthetic-sermon **data engine is independently fundable** (it also generates the eval corpus and Stage-2 SFT data) and is the most reusable asset in the whole P3 program.

**Test-time adaptation (the missing common answer to per-venue fragility):** a 5-minute pre-service calibration — the speaker (or last week's recording) reads a few manuscript lines; fit per-speaker prosody norms, ASR biasing, and the threshold operating point on the spot. Every knob-exposing proposal inherits "tuned on service A, mis-fires on service B"; a cheap calibration ritual is the obvious answer none of them include.

## 6. The single highest-upside frontier bet

**A dedicated predictive prosodic-boundary sensor, fused as a second independent vote with the lexical/semantic position sensor, both taught by the operator-correction flywheel (#P + #O on top of #1/#3).**

I deliberately do **not** pick a from-scratch transducer or a 7B-scale GRPO program as the bet. Verification makes the case against them blunt: the purest action-lane models (Families 1, 3, 4) are XL-effort, depend on unsolved long-horizon sparse-reward RL or unproven manuscript-conditioning, and their nearest proof-point lands *at or below* the cascade you can build now. They are the right *eventual on-prem end-state* gated behind a product decision — not the bet that moves Seeker.

The prosodic sensor is the highest *risk-adjusted* frontier move because it is the **only** mechanism that attacks the two structurally-unsolved sub-problems head-on:
- **Predictive firing.** Worship/song mode needs the slide up **~1 s before** the line is sung. Every content-matcher — cascade, frozen head, even a duplex action lane — is structurally **reactive**: the words arrive at or after the instant the slide must already be on screen. Prosody *leads* content. No amount of model scale fixes a reactive sensor's inability to anticipate. This is a genuine capability gap, not a tuning deficit, and it likely means **song mode needs a fundamentally different mechanism than sermon mode** (the critic's scope question — resolve it before committing, because it changes which proposals are even applicable to half the use case).
- **Identical-text disambiguation.** Chorus repeats and jump-back-to-reread are *content-indistinguishable*; the locality prior — borrowed wholesale into ~10 proposals — *breaks* on legitimate non-local jumps (skip back to chorus 1 from the bridge), and the tight-vs-loose-prior tradeoff is unwinnable with a single prior. The disambiguating signal lives **outside** the content+locality frame, in delivery.

It is cheap (CPU-feasible small CNN/Conformer, or even classical pitch-reset/pause-onset features), trainable from the operator flywheel without verbatim labels, and degrades independently of ASR WER on noisy PA — orthogonal failure modes, which is exactly what you want in a second voter.

**Minimal proof-of-concept (2–4 weeks):**
1. From Stage-0 recorded services + gold fire-times, extract a window around each true transition; label "boundary" vs "interior."
2. Train a small classifier on log-mel + f0 + energy + speaking-rate features to predict boundary-imminent **0.5–1.5 s ahead**. Hold out by speaker and by venue.
3. Measure **lead-time distribution** and **boundary precision/recall**, *especially on chorus repeats and on song-mode lines* (where content matchers are blind).
4. Fuse as a vote with the Stage-1 cascade posterior; measure the delta in song-mode fire-offset (target: net *negative*/predictive offset) and chorus-disambiguation accuracy vs. cascade-alone.

**Kill signal:** if prosodic features give no usable lead-time or no chorus separation beyond the content channel, drop it and accept that song mode requires the operator-confirm path (#O) as the primary mechanism — itself a clean, shippable answer. **Win signal:** any reliable pre-line lead + chorus separation makes this the cheapest path to the two things nothing else can do.

## 7. Open research questions & honest risks

**Sharpest open questions (answer empirically, in order):**
1. **Quantify the cost asymmetry.** Is a 1.5 s-late advance truly invisible? Is a one-line-ahead wrong-local slide as bad as a wild jump or far less? The entire firing policy (eager vs. conservative, abstain-vs-fire) is *derived* from this, and it is currently asserted, not measured.
2. **Does song mode need a different architecture than sermon mode?** If predictive prosody is required for worship, a single sermon-optimized model is the wrong frame for half the use case. Resolve scope before committing.
3. **Can a frozen hidden state linearly separate boundary-crossing AND correct local index under paraphrase/chorus?** The 1–2 week linear probe that cheaply kills or promotes the *entire* frozen-head family (#4/#5/#9/#10/#12).
4. **Is the cascade's ceiling actually set by ASR WER, and how much does a clean console feed + biasing + AEC raise it?** The cascade is the baseline everything must beat; its true ceiling is the unmeasured audio front-end.
5. **Will operators tolerate propose-confirm, and do their corrections yield clean on-policy labels?** The flywheel — the best data answer — only works if engagement and attribution both hold.
6. **Is slide-level the right granularity, or should the system track sub-slide (line/phrase) position and emit "cross-into-next-block" events?** Sub-slide tracking changes the label problem, the latency budget, and makes prosodic anticipation natural.

**Cross-cutting risks (these bind the whole field, not any one proposal):**
- **The safety net is reactive and poll-blind.** Verified in code: reconcile is `sleep(interval)`-paced and re-anchors only after an observed change (daemon.py:388–389); locality bounds only wild jumps. A plausible-but-wrong *local* slide — the actual failure mode — is on the projector before correction. Do not let any design lean on `evaluate_trigger`/reconcile as *preventive*. A push-based or event-driven slide-state feed would materially help, if ProPresenter exposes one.
- **Noisy live-PA degrades every input channel at once.** ASR WER, audio-LLM hidden states, and Mimi tokens all collapse together in a reverberant sanctuary with monitor bleed and a music bed. There is no acoustic backstop common across proposals. The front-end (console feed, AEC, source separation) is the most underweighted lever relative to impact — and the prosodic sensor's WER-independence is its one partial hedge.
- **Sparse-emission training instability is a symptom, not a bug to patch.** ~0.1% positive frames means focal/blank-downweight/anchor-loss are band-aids on the decision to use a *dense per-frame* objective for a *rare-event* problem. Event-triggered / boundary-gated computation (only spend the expensive sensors *near* a predicted boundary — the critic's active-sensing idea) is barely explored and is the principled fix; it also slashes the wasted compute of running a head every 80 ms for 90 minutes to fire 30 times.
- **Long-session (60–90 min) stability is demonstrated by nobody.** Cloud: sliding-window compression silently erodes positional memory. Open backbones: short-turn-trained, "fires correctly after 80 min of mostly-silence" unshown. Cascade: stateless per-window re-anchoring can drift on repeated text. This is hoped across the board; the eval harness must include an hour-scale stability test.
- **Forced alignment is tool-availability driving design.** ≥8 proposals depend on it and all concede it's noisiest on the only cases that matter. The operator flywheel + cascade-distillation are the escape; do not build a trained head on forced-alignment labels alone.

**Honest framing of the verdict.** The deployable answer (Stage 1) is *not* a frontier result — it is paradigm (c) from Seeker's own research doc, executed well, and it satisfies A/B/C and acceptably D today. The genuine frontier contribution available at sane cost is **splitting the sensor problem**: a predictive prosodic channel + an on-policy operator-teacher flywheel + an explicit off-script latent, fused into the deterministic posterior. The purest "duplex model" (a trained silent action lane on a Mimi/DuplexSLA-style backbone) remains the right *long-horizon on-prem end-state* and the right thing to keep the **L3 serving substrate** ready for — but it is gated behind a product decision and a measured demonstration that the cascade is genuinely insufficient, and its own evidence says its accuracy ceiling sits at-or-below the cascade. Build the instrument, ship the cascade, split the sensors, let the operator teach — and treat the from-scratch action lane as a research bet with a watch-trigger, not a delivery plan.
